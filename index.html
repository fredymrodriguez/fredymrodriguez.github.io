Fredy Mendieta-Rodriguez

Ph.D. Candidate in Hispanic Linguistics

I am a trilingual educator (Spanish, English, French) and linguist specializing in embodied cognition, sociolinguistics, phonetics, and lexicography of Spanish. I am passionate about creating inclusive, engaging learning environments that foster critical thinking and deep language acquisition.

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy is grounded in the belief that language learning is a transformative process that connects learners to new cultures and perspectives. I am committed to creating inclusive, student-centered learning environments where every learner feels valued, supported, and empowered.

A major focus of my teaching is developing metalinguistic awareness—helping students shed linguistic shame and recognize that every dialect is legitimate, meaningful, and deeply connected to community and identity.
