Fredy Mendieta-Rodriguez

Ph.D. Candidate in Hispanic Linguistics

I am a trilingual educator (Spanish, English, French) and linguist specializing in embodied cognition, sociolinguistics, phonetics, and lexicography of Spanish[cite: 1]. I am currently completing my Ph.D. at The University of New Mexico[cite: 1].

My work is driven by a passion for inclusive learning environments, critical thinking, and the intersection of linguistic theory and real-world applications.

Teaching Philosophy

Teaching is a deeply transformative experience that goes beyond delivering information; it is the creation of a learning space where students feel seen, valued, and intellectually challenged[cite: 2]. Inclusion and social justice are foundational to my approach[cite: 2].

I aim to develop metalinguistic awareness, helping students shed linguistic shame and recognize that every dialect is legitimate and deeply connected to community and identity[cite: 2]. My assessments focus on authentic research applications rather than traditional exams[cite: 2].
